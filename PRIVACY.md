# Privacy Policy for Ragnarok Launcher

**Last updated:** October 1, 2026

Ragnarok Launcher is an open-source application committed to respecting your privacy.

### 1. Data Collection
Ragnarok Launcher **does not collect, store, or sell any personal identifying information** on external servers.

### 2. Local Storage
- Game configurations, save backup archives, and application preferences are stored strictly locally on your computer (in your local AppData folder).
- Cloud backups to Google Drive are made directly from your computer to your personal Google Drive account using Google's official API. No credentials or files pass through any intermediary third-party server.

### 3. Network Requests
- The application connects directly to public APIs (such as Steam and GitHub) solely to retrieve game metadata, patch information, and launcher updates.

### 4. Open Source Transparency
All source code is publicly available and verifiable under the **GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0)** at [https://github.com/RagnarokManifests/ragnarok-launcher](https://github.com/RagnarokManifests/ragnarok-launcher).
